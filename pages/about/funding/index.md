Open Research Network is developing a sustainable support model for projects,
programs, and community activities. ORN does **not** currently have a direct
fiscal sponsor. Instead, access to fiscal and financial administration is
coordinated through Open Science Labs (OSL) partnerships.

## Access paths

Different initiatives may need different administrative structures:

### Open-source projects

Eligible open-source projects may access fiscal sponsorship through **Open
Source Collective** via the Open Science Labs ecosystem. This pathway is most
appropriate for open-source software, open infrastructure, and related community
work that fits Open Source Collective's scope.

### Non-open-source and broader research initiatives

For research initiatives that are not open-source projects, or that need a
different administrative arrangement, financial management may be coordinated
with **The GRAPH Network** through the OSL partnership ecosystem.

## Why this matters

Many scientific communities need help with practical operations: receiving
funds, paying contributors, supporting events, maintaining infrastructure, and
coordinating services. These access paths allow ORN-related initiatives to seek
support without implying that ORN itself currently operates a direct fiscal
sponsorship structure.

## Fees and conditions

Fees, eligibility, reporting requirements, and administrative conditions depend
on the selected pathway and the nature of the project. These details should be
made explicit before a grant proposal, sponsorship agreement, or funding request
is submitted.

## Grant proposals and sponsored projects

Projects that plan to use one of these pathways should describe the relevant
OSL partnership access path clearly in proposals and budgets. Collaborators who
will be paid through a financial management structure should have their roles,
responsibilities, and payment conditions documented in advance.

## Commitment to transparency

ORN aims to keep funding arrangements understandable for contributors,
affiliates, partners, and sponsors. As the network grows, we will continue to
improve documentation around fiscal processes, financial management, project
support, and sustainable operations.
