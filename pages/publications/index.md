ORN will use this space to highlight research outputs from affiliated people,
organizations, projects, and partners.

## What will appear here

This section is being prepared as a home for:

- publications from affiliated research groups;
- reports, white papers, and technical notes;
- open datasets and reproducible research artifacts;
- project documentation with scientific relevance;
- community outputs from events, workshops, and collaborations.

## How we think about publication

For ORN, publication is not limited to journal articles. Open research also
includes reusable software, documented workflows, datasets, educational
materials, protocols, and community reports. We want this page to make those
outputs easier to discover and cite.

## Coming next

As the network grows, we will define a lightweight submission and curation
process so affiliated groups can share outputs in a consistent, trustworthy way.

If your organization is interested in sharing open research outputs through ORN,
we invite you to connect with us.
